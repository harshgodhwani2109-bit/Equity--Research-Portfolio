# Equity--Research-Portfolio
Welcome to my financial modeling and equity research repository. This portfolio showcases institutional-grade 3-statement financial models, discounted cash flow (DCF) valuations, relative peer comparisons, and comprehensive equity research reports developed for Indian corporations across the FMCG and Construction Materials sectors.

* 1.) Britannia Industries Limited (FMCG Industry) For Britannia Industries, an integrated 10-year financial model (FY27E–FY36E) and valuation methodology were developed.
 Important Elements Constructed:
3-Statement Model: Full Income Statement, Balance Sheet, and Cash Flow forecasts in a Base Case scenario, accounting for quick-commerce scaling and rural FMCG recovery.
  Standardized operational ratios (COGS, salaries, and SG&A expenses as a percentage of revenue) based on past patterns (FY22–FY26) are used in the cost and margin analysis.
  Valuation Framework: WACC build-up using CAPM, built DCF (Perpetuity Growth & Exit Multiple methods), and relative peer benchmarking against Nestlé India, Bikaji Foods, and Mrs. Bectors across EV/EBITDA, EV/Sales, and P/E multiples. Deliverables: Excel

  2. UltraTech Cement Limited (Sector of Construction Materials) Overview: Developed a thorough valuation model and equity research report for the biggest cement producer in India.
* Important Elements Constructed:
* Capacity & Volume Modeling: Gray cement capacity scaling beyond 200 MTPA and multi-year capital extension pipelines up to 240+ MTPA were examined.
   Operational Metrics: Net debt-to-EBITDA deleveraging trajectories, captive renewable energy cost savings, and EBITDA-per-tonne improvements were also assessed.
  Valuation & Sensitivity: Conducted thorough peer comparisons and sensitivity analyzes against seven competitors in the industry (Ambuja Cement, Shree Cement, JK Cement, Dalmia Bharat, ACC, and The Ramco Cements).
